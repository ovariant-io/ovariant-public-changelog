# Changelog

August release

Empowering knowledge and connection! We're excited to introduce our new article publication system designed to enhance Ovariant's public knowledge space. This release focuses on creating valuable content experiences for women and building an organized platform for knowledge sharing.


## 2025-08-17

### Article Publication System
- Launched comprehensive content management platform for internal publishing
- Developed engaging article display and organization system
- Implemented content categorization and search functionality
- Created user-friendly reading experience with responsive design

### Knowledge Space Enhancement
- Built structured content library focused on providing value to women
- Established content curation workflows for quality assurance
- Implemented content discovery features for improved accessibility

### Content Management Features
- Internal member publishing dashboard with intuitive interface
- Article drafting and editing tools with collaborative features
- Content scheduling and publication workflow management
- Analytics and engagement tracking for published content

### Platform Improvements
- Enhanced content delivery and performance optimization
- Improved mobile responsiveness for article consumption
- Streamlined navigation for better content discovery
- Added accessibility features for inclusive content access

### Major Milestone
🎉 **Article Publication System is now live!** 
Our internal team can now publish valuable content that reaches and empowers women in our community. This marks a significant step in building Ovariant's knowledge-sharing ecosystem.
